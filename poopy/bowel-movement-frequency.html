Is it normal to poop more than once a day?

Yes, more than one bowel movement a day can be part of an adult’s usual pattern. In a US study of adults who described their bowel habits as normal, 95.9% reported between three and 21 bowel movements a week. That range describes a group of people. Your own usual pattern is a better starting point for describing a change. Read the population study.

Does three times in one day tell me much?

The count tells you how many times you went. It leaves out timing, stool shape and how you felt. Three formed bowel movements over a whole day and three loose bowel movements in one hour would both appear as “3” on a calendar. A diary can preserve that difference.

How do I compare one week with another?

First, check which days you actually recorded. For example, six visits across three recorded days average two visits per recorded day. If the other four days are blank, you still don’t know the full week’s average. Mark a day as zero only if you know you had no bowel movement.

When explaining a change, a sentence like “I usually go once in the morning, but this week I went again after lunch on three days” gives more detail than a weekly total. If a change persists or worries you, discuss it with a healthcare professional.
