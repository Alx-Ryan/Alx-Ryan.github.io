What’s the best poop tracker app for iPhone?

If you want quick logging, a calendar you can look back through, and a little bathroom humor, try Poopy. It’s a free poop tracker and bowel movement diary for iPhone and iPad. The optional Theme Pack adds bathroom buddies like a suspicious cat on a toilet.

Can you log more than one poop a day?

Yes. Say you go before breakfast at 7:15 and again after lunch at 2:40. In Poopy, tap I pooped! each time and save the entries separately. Open that day in History to review both times and any notes you added. Try that two-visit example in any app you’re considering to see how easy it is to find both entries.

Does it have a Bristol stool chart?

Yes. You can save a stool shape using the seven Bristol types. If you’re choosing between nearby types, like a smooth log or separate soft pieces, use the illustrated Bristol stool chart to compare them. You can also add a note when you want to remember more about a visit.

What can you do for free?

You can log bowel movements, browse your calendar, and export a PDF of your history. If you want to share a specific week, open History, choose Export under the tools button, and select your dates and the fields you want to include.

What makes Poopy fun?

The optional Theme Pack is a one-time purchase. It lets you decorate the Log screen and change the app icon. You can give your diary a golden throne or pick a suspicious ginger cat clutching a toilet roll.
