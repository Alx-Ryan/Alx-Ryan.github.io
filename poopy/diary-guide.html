How do I keep a bowel movement diary?

Write down the date, time and stool shape each time you have a bowel movement. Start a new row if you go again that day. A short note is enough for anything else you want to remember.

What if I go twice during one visit?

For a personal diary, you can record one continuous bathroom visit as one entry and describe both parts in the note. If you leave and come back later for another bowel movement, make a second entry. Use the same convention when you compare days. If your clinician supplied a diary, follow its instructions.

What goes in the notes?

Record details you think you might otherwise forget, such as “felt rushed” or “away from home.” If you track meals or coffee too, write their times separately. Coffee at 8 am and a bathroom visit at 10 am are two different observations. Recording their order helps you describe the day without guessing what caused a change.

What if I miss a day?

“No bowel movement” and “I don’t remember” mean different things. Write whichever is true when looking back. If the time is approximate, write “around lunchtime.” A blank day alone doesn’t tell you what happened.
