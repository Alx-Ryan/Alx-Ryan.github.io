Privacy Policy – Poopy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Home Support

This policy explains how Poopy handles your logs, app usage, and purchases.

1. Logs, iCloud, and widgets

Poopy keeps your logs on your device. If iCloud is available, iCloud Sync is on by default and can sync your logs, photos, notes, tags, and app settings through your Apple iCloud account. Change this in Poopy’s Settings under iCloud Sync.

In version 2.0.0, Poopy works out which trophies you’ve earned on your device. The Daily Rhythm widget uses recent dates and daily log counts stored on the device so it can draw your history. The widget’s summary stays on your device.

2. Optional product analytics

In version 2.0.0, Poopy sends product analytics only if you enable it. Mixpanel processes app usage events such as app opens, onboarding progress, screen views, selected button taps, export use, Theme Pack activity, and app errors. The events include a randomly generated identifier and technical information such as your app version, device model, and operating system. We use this information to understand app usage and fix problems.

Poopy does not send bowel logs, dates or counts of your bowel movements, stool types, symptoms, notes, tags, photos, or Apple Health data to Mixpanel. Earlier versions could send an event saying a log was created when analytics was enabled. Version 2.0.0 removes that event.

Turn off product analytics in Poopy’s Settings to stop sending new events. Turning it off does not delete events that were already sent. Contact us to request deletion of information we hold about you. Mixpanel Privacy Policy

3. App Store purchases

Apple processes Theme Pack purchases and restores. Apple can send purchase and refund notifications to RevenueCat, our purchase reporting provider. These notifications can include a product identifier, transaction identifier, and transaction date. We use them to track sales and refunds. RevenueCat receives the notifications from Apple without access to your Poopy logs or Apple Health data. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Apple Health

Apple Health sync is off until you turn it on and grant permission. With permission, Poopy can write selected bowel events mapped from Bristol types 1–2 and 6–7 and the caffeine amount you choose to log with the coffee quick tag. You can separately grant access to sleep, hydration, caffeine, and medication data for insights in Poopy. We don’t sell your health data or use it for advertising.

You can change these permissions or delete data already written to Apple Health in the Health app.

5. Your records and choices

Your logs remain in Poopy until you delete them. You can edit or delete individual entries and export a copy from History. Manage copies you’ve exported or shared wherever you saved or sent them. Turning off iCloud Sync stops future syncing. It does not delete copies already stored in iCloud.

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to respond. Contact us with privacy questions or deletion requests. Support-AlexRyanDev@pm.me

Poopy is a wellness logging app and is not a medical device. Content in the app is informational and should not replace professional medical advice.
