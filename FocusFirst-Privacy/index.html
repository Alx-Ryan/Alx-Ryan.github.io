Privacy Policy – Focus First

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Home Support

Focus First is a productivity app designed to help users reduce distracting screen time and build better focus habits.

1. Data Storage

  • Focus settings, rules, goals, and app selections are stored locally on your device.
  • If iCloud Sync is enabled, data may be synced through your personal iCloud account.
  • We do not operate our own servers for storing your Screen Time activity data.

2. Screen Time Permissions

  • Focus First uses Apple's Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings frameworks to help block selected apps and measure focus progress.
  • Usage information is accessed only after you grant permission.
  • This information is used only for app functionality such as blocking distractions, tracking focus progress, and calculating rewards.
  • Screen Time data remains on-device and is not sold or shared with advertisers.

3. Analytics

  • Analytics are disabled by default and only enabled if you choose to allow them.
  • Analytics may include anonymous events such as screen views, onboarding completion, button taps, subscription events, and feature usage.
  • We do not collect app names, detailed Screen Time activity, messages, photos, browsing history, or private content for analytics.

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

  • Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
  • We do not receive or store your payment card information.
  • Subscription status may be verified through Apple's systems to unlock premium features.

5. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share Screen Time activity data with advertisers.
  • Information may only be disclosed when required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the app.

6. User Controls

  • You can change or revoke Screen Time permissions through your device settings.
  • You may delete app data by removing the app or clearing stored data where available.
  • You can disable analytics at any time within the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Focus First is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by revising the “Last updated” date above.
