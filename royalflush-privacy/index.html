Privacy Policy – Poopy

Last updated: February 8, 2026

Home Support

Poopy is built with privacy first. Your bowel logs are personal and sensitive.

1. Data Storage

  • Logs are stored locally on your device.
  • If iCloud Sync is enabled, your data is synced using your iCloud account.

2. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your health-related data.
  • Product analytics is off by default and only turns on after you allow it.
  • Analytics is limited to anonymous screen views, button taps, onboarding progress, log creation, and export use.
  • No bowel logs, Bristol type, symptoms, notes, tags, photos, or other health details are sent to analytics.

3. Apple Health Sync

  • Apple Health sync is off by default and only runs if you enable it.
  • Poopy syncs events mapped to Bristol 1-2 and 6-7.
  • If you use the Home quick tag for coffee/caffeine and pick an amount, that caffeine amount is also synced.
  • Notes, photos, tags, and other Bristol entries stay in Poopy.

4. User Controls

  • You can edit or delete entries at any time.
  • You can export your data from History.

5. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact support.

Poopy is a wellness logging app and is not a medical device. Content in the app is informational and should not replace professional medical advice.
