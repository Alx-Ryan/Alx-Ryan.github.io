Privacy Policy – Focus First

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Home Support

Focus First helps you reduce distracting screen time and build better focus habits. The most important privacy point is simple: your Screen Time data stays on your iPhone.

1. Screen Time Data Stays On Device

  • Focus First uses Apple's Screen Time, Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings APIs to help block distractions and measure focus progress.
  • Your Screen Time history, app names, website names, selected app/category/domain tokens, rules, and reward balances stay on your device.
  • This data is used for app features such as blocking selected apps, tracking progress, and calculating rewards.
  • Focus First does not send your Screen Time history, app names, website names, selected tokens, rules, or reward balances to our servers or to analytics providers.

2. Permissions

  • Focus First only accesses Screen Time features after you grant permission through Apple's permission screens.
  • You can change or revoke Screen Time permissions in your device settings.
  • Focus First does not require an account or an email address to use the app.

3. Product Analytics

Focus First uses PostHog for limited product analytics so we can understand what is working and improve the app. This is separate from Screen Time data.

  • Analytics may help us understand onboarding performance, paywall performance, purchases, daily retention, and feature usage.
  • Analytics may include event names, timestamps, general device and app metadata, product or paywall metadata, and an anonymous persistent analytics identifier.
  • Analytics may include count-only totals, such as how many apps were selected, but not the names of selected apps or websites.
  • Focus First does not use analytics for advertising, retargeting, data brokers, or tracking you across other apps or websites.

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

  • Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
  • We do not receive or store your payment card information.
  • Subscription status may be verified through Apple's systems to unlock premium features.

5. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share Screen Time data with advertisers or analytics providers.
  • We do not use analytics data for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Information may be disclosed only when required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the app.

6. User Controls

  • You can change or revoke Screen Time permissions through your device settings.
  • You may delete app data by removing the app or clearing stored data where available.

7. Children's Privacy

Focus First is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.
