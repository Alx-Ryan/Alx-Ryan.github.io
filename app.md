## Core Mechanic (from screenshots)

1. **Block distracting apps** (Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, YouTube, Facebook…) behind a Screen Time shield.
2. **Focus on productive apps** (study, language learning, reading, work) — a **Focus Engine** converts focus time into earned scroll time at an adjustable rate (e.g. 10m focus → 3m scroll).
3. **Spend earned time** to unlock blocked apps ("You've earned 43m for this app. Spend 5m to unlock now.").
4. **Track progress** — daily insights, weekly trends, focus totals, streaks, multipliers, focus score.
